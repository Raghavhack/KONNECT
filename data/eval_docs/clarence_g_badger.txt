Clarence G. Badger
Clarence G. Badger( June 9, 1880 – June 17, 1964) was an American film director of feature films in the 1910s, 1920s and 1930s. His films include" It" and" Red Hair", more than a dozen features and shorts starring Will Rogers, and two features starring Raymond Griffith," Paths to Paradise" and" Hands Up!" He moved to Australia to direct" Rangle River"( 1936) and decided to retire there, only making one more feature," That Certain Something"( 1941).
