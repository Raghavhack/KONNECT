Bersvend Martinussen Røkkum
Bersvend Martinussen Røkkum (2 February 1806 – 14 August 1867) was a Norwegian politician. He was elected to the Norwegian Parliament in 1842, representing the rural constituency of "Romsdals Amt" (today named Møre og Romsdal). He worked as a farmer. He served only one term. He was born and died in Ålvundfjord.
