Bea Saw
Maria Beatriz Imperial Saw- Tan( née Saw; born November 17, 1985), known as Beatriz Saw or Bea Saw, is a Filipino actress with Taiwanese descent. She won the second season of" Pinoy Big Brother". On June 30, 2007( day 126) after garnering about 1.5 million votes, or 30.29% of the total number of votes, Saw was proclaimed as the Big Winner of" Pinoy Big Brother". Her record erased Kim Chiu's record for the most votes earned by a Big Winner. She received a million pesos, and prizes including a house and lot, a kitchen showcase, a new car, a business franchise and an Asian package tour for two.
