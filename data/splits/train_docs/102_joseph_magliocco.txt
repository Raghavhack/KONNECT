Joseph Magliocco
Joseph Magliocco( born Giuseppe Magliocco;; June 29, 1898 – December 28, 1963), also known as" Joe Malayak" and" Joe Evil Eye", was an Italian- born New York mobster and the boss of the Profaci crime family( later to become the Colombo crime family) from 1962 to 1963. Magliocco participated in an audacious unsuccessful attempt to kill other family bosses and take over the Mafia Commission.
