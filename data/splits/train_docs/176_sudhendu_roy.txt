Sudhendu Roy
Sudhendu Roy (1921–1999) was a noted Indian film director, art director and production designer in Hindi cinema, most known for his realistic art direction in auteur Bimal Roy's films, like "Sujata" (1959), "Madhumati" (1959) and "Bandini" (1963), and glitzy work in films Subhash Ghai's "Karz" (1980) and "Karma" (1986) to Yash Chopra's "Silsila" (1981), "Chandni" (1989) and "Lamhe" (1991). He won the Filmfare Award for Best Art Direction thrice for, "Madhumati" (1959), "Mere Mehboob" (1964) and "Sagina" (1975). He also directed Hindi films like "Uphaar" (1971) and "Saudagar" (1973), both of which were India's official entry to the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film.
