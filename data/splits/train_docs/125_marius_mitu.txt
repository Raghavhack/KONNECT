Marius Mitu
Marius Mitu (born 10 September 1976) is a retired Romanian midfielder who last played for plays for CSM Râmnicu Vâlcea. Mitu has played for R.S.C. Anderlecht until February 2006 and has appeared at Lierse SK previously. Mitu also played for R.W.D. Molenbeek (one year) and R.A.A. Louviéroise (six months) in Belgium. Prior to his move to Belgium in 2001, he played for several Romanian clubs such as Steaua Bucureşti and FC Universitatea Craiova. In February 2006, Mitu was expelled from Anderlecht, because of his possible connection with a match-fixing scandal when he played for Lierse SK in 2005.
