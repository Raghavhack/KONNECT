Ognen Stojanovski
Ognen Stojanovski( born January 25, 1984) is a Macedonian professional basketball player. He was under contract with MZT Skopje until 2014. He is 1.90 m( 6 ft 3 in) in height and plays at the point guard position. Born in Skopje, Republic of Macedonia, he is older brother of the twins Vojdan Stojanovski and Damjan Stojanovski, who are also basketball players.
