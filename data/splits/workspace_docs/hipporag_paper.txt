HippoRAG: Neurobiologically Inspired Long-Term Memory for Large Language Models
Abstract: Human long-term memory relies on the hippocampal-neocortical system to perform pattern completion and associative retrieval. Current RAG systems lack associative multi-hop retrieval capabilities across disparate memory chunks. HippoRAG models hippocampal indexing by constructing an open-information extraction knowledge graph from documents. When presented with a complex query, HippoRAG computes Personalized PageRank (PPR) over entity nodes, diffusing query activation across associative paths in the graph. This activates indirectly related evidence passages that vector similarity alone misses. HippoRAG achieves high recall on multi-hop benchmarks with a fraction of the index rebuild latency.
