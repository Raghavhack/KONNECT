G-Retriever: Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Textual Graph Understanding and Reasoning
Abstract: Graph Neural Networks combined with Large Language Models face scalability bottlenecks when handling large textual graphs. G-Retriever proposes a targeted subgraph retrieval paradigm based on the Prize-Collecting Steiner Tree (PCST) optimization problem. Rather than injecting the entire graph or arbitrary k-hop neighbors into the LLM context, G-Retriever formulates query-guided node prizes derived from cosine similarities between question embeddings and node representations. Edge costs represent relational traversal penalties. The PCST algorithm extracts a minimal connected subgraph that maximizes total prize while minimizing edge costs. Retrieved subgraphs are linearized and soft-prompted into LLM encoders, improving multi-hop graph QA accuracy.
