Joan Ramon II, Count of Cardona
The 3rd Count of Cardona, Joan Ramon II Folc de Cardona (14 June 14001471), was a Catalan nobleman in the late Middle Ages. HIs titles included Count of Cardona and Count-consort of Prades, as well as Viscount of Vilamur. ' Juan Ramón Folch de Cardona y Ximenez de Arenós' His parents were Joan Ramon I, 2nd Count of Cardona, and his wife Joana de Gandia.
