Noemie Fox
Noemie Fox (born 19 March 1997) is an Australian slalom canoeist who has competed at the international level since 2013. She won two medals in the C1 team event at the ICF Canoe Slalom World Championships with a gold in 2019 ICF Canoe Slalom World Championships and a silver in 2017 ICF Canoe Slalom World Championships. Noemie comes from a canoe slalom family with her father being Richard Fox, her mother Myriam Fox-Jerusalmi, her aunt Rachel Crosbee and her older sister Jessica Fox.
