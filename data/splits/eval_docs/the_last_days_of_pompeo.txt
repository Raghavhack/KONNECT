The Last Days of Pompeo
The Last Days of Pompeo( Italian: Gli ultimi giorni di Pompeo) is a 1937 Italian comedy film directed by Mario Mattoli and starring Enrico Viarisio, Roberta Mari and Camillo Pilotto. The film's title is an allusion to the novel" The Last Days of Pompeii" by Edward Bulwer- Lytton. It refers to a character in contemporary Italy named Pompeo. The film's was shot at Cinecittà Studios in Rome.
