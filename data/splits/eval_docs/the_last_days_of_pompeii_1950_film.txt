The Last Days of Pompeii (1950 film)
The Last Days of Pompeii( 1950) is a black and white French- Italian drama film, directed by Marcel L'Herbier" in collaboration with" Paolo Moffa, who was also the director of production. It was adapted from Edward Bulwer- Lytton's novel" The Last Days of Pompeii". The film has also been known as" Sins of Pompeii".
