Matt Joyce (baseball)
Matthew Ryan Joyce( born August 3, 1984) is an American professional baseball outfielder who is a free agent. He has played in Major League Baseball( MLB) for the Detroit Tigers, Tampa Bay Rays, Los Angeles Angels of Anaheim, Pittsburgh Pirates, Oakland Athletics and Atlanta Braves. Joyce was an All- Star selection in 2011. Joyce and his wife, Brittany, have a daughter, Mackenzie Joyce.
