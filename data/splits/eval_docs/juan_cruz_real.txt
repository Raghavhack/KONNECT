Juan Cruz Real
Juan Cruz Real( born October 8, 1976) is a former Argentine footballer currently hired as a Club Head Technical Coach/ Technical Director for Mississauga Soccer Club in Canada. He also works for Argentinean Football Coaches Association responsible for the management of the ATFA International Development Program. He had numerous stints in South American leagues, and finished off his career in the Canadian Professional Soccer League. He is currently the manager of Alianza Petrolera.
