Princess Caroline of Waldeck and Pyrmont
Caroline Louise of Waldeck and Pyrmont( 14 August 1748 – 18 August 1782), was a Princess of Waldeck and Pyrmont by birth and Duchess consort of Courland by marriage. She was the daughter of Karl August, Prince of Waldeck and Pyrmont, and Countess Palatine Christiane Henriette of Zweibrücken- Birkenfeld.
