Chiara Caselli
Chiara Caselli( born 22 December 1967) is an Italian actress. In 1994, Caselli won the Nastro d' Argento for Best Actress award as well as the David di Donatello for Best Actress award. Caselli has appeared in American films including" My Own Private Idaho" opposite River Phoenix and Keanu Reeves. In 2002 she also appeared opposite John Malkovich in" Ripley's Game".
