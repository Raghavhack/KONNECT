Friedrich Anton Ulrich, Prince of Waldeck and Pyrmont
Friedrich Anton Ulrich, Prince of Waldeck and Pyrmont( 27 November 16761 January 1728) was the first reigning Prince of Waldeck and Pyrmont from 1712 to 1728. He was the son of Christian Louis, Count of Waldeck and Countess Anna Elisabeth of Rappoltstein. From 1706 to 1712 he was Count of Waldeck and Pyrmont. On 6 January 1712 he was elevated to Prince by the Emperor Charles VI.
