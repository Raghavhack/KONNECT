Louis George, Margrave of Baden-Baden
Louis George, Margrave of Baden-Baden (7 June 1702 – 22 October 1761) was the Margrave of Baden-Baden from 1707 until his death in 1761. From 1707 to 1727, his mother Sibylle of Saxe-Lauenburg was the regent of Baden-Baden. He was succeeded by his younger brother, Augustus George. Because of his passion for hunting, he was nicknamed "Jägerlouis" (the "hunter Louis").
