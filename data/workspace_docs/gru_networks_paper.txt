Empirical Evaluation of Gated Recurrent Neural Networks on Sequence Modeling
Abstract: Recurrent Neural Networks often suffer from vanishing and exploding gradients when learning long-term temporal dependencies. The Gated Recurrent Unit (GRU) offers an efficient alternative to Long Short-Term Memory (LSTM) cells. A GRU cell contains two gating primitives: an update gate z and a reset gate r. The reset gate determines how much of the previous memory state to discard, while the update gate regulates how much of the candidate activation is committed to the new hidden state. Having fewer parameters than LSTMs, GRUs train faster while achieving comparable performance on sequential and streaming temporal state tracking.
